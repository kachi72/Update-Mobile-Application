# Plan 1-002 — Saved Articles and Loading States

**Area:** Android UI / Saved Articles / Loading states  
**Priority:** P1  
**Depends On:** Plan 1-001

**Objective**

Bring Saved Articles and the online and offline loading states into the briefing design system introduced by Plan 1-001 while preserving existing saved-article persistence and article-loading behavior.

**Acceptance Criteria**

The Saved Articles screen uses a soft lavender surface and a rounded gradient header.  
The header displays an accurate saved-article count and supporting copy.  
Saved articles appear as spacious rounded cards using the gradient associated with their category.  
Each saved card presents category, title, description, and date with a clear visual hierarchy.  
Home, Offline, and Saved navigation actions remain available, with Saved presented as the active destination.  
The empty state appears when no articles are saved and updates immediately after the final saved article is removed.  
Article open and long-press removal actions use the current adapter position safely.  
Long-press removal is consumed and presents a clear confirmation dialog.  
The online and offline Lottie views are replaced by compact indeterminate Material progress indicators on elevated rounded surfaces.  
Existing loading container IDs and Activity visibility behavior remain compatible.  
SharedPreferences persistence, WorkManager behavior, Room behavior, and WebView navigation remain unchanged.  
The debug application builds successfully and local unit tests pass.

**Done When**

Saved Articles and both loading states visually match the redesigned application, saved-item counts and empty-state transitions remain accurate, and existing article workflows continue without regression.

**Validation**

Run `./gradlew testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug` from the project root.  
Verify the populated and empty Saved Articles states, category card colors, article opening, long-press removal, saved-item counts, bottom navigation, and online and offline loading indicators on a device or emulator.
